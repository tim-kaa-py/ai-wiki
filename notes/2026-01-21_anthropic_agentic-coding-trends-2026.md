## Confirmed Discoveries

- The "collaboration paradox" stat: 60% of work uses AI but only 0–20% is "fully delegated" — the report's central counter-narrative against full-autonomy framing.
- 27% of AI-assisted work is *net-new* (nice-to-haves, papercuts, exploratory work that wouldn't have happened manually) — productivity reframed as output volume, not just speed per task.
- Rakuten / vLLM case: 7 hours autonomous, 12.5M LOC repo, 99.9% numerical accuracy — concrete data point on long-running agent reliability.
- Three multipliers framing: agent capabilities × orchestration × human experience compound to *step-function*, not linear, gains.
- Single-orchestrator + parallel specialist sub-agents pattern named as the dominant multi-agent architecture (Fountain Copilot as exemplar).
- Onboarding compression (weeks → hours) reframed as enabling dynamic "surge staffing" — talent-deployment, not just dev-velocity, framing.
- "Everyone becomes more full-stack" — the report's framing for the role transformation: humans broaden, not deepen, because AI fills knowledge gaps.
